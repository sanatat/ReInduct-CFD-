# Source register and experimental scope

Hidayat, T.; Rhamdhani, M. A.; Jak, E.; Hayes, P. C.,
"The Kinetics of Reduction of Dense Synthetic Nickel Oxide in H2-N2 and H2-H2O Atmospheres",
*Metallurgical and Materials Transactions B* **40** (2009) 1–16.
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/s11663-008-9212-0

The local CSV transcribes **Table III** (initial growth rate of the *Ni metal layer* over the first 0–5 s in H2-N2 gas at 1 atm). It records 19 experimental mean rates with reported standard deviations. Units: um/s. The test specimens were dense synthetic NiO, **not** porous mixed spent-battery black mass. All 19 values are reproduced individually for scientific analysis, not an extraction of an entire article.

Paper context: H2 pressure dependence approximately linear initially; rate has a non-monotonic T response (especially 675 to 800 deg C). Validation of that observable **does not** validate intrinsic porous-bed rate coefficients, a reactor, or black-mass recovery. Extraction is checked against source Table III; NO extra points synthesized.

Additional mechanistic context (NOT used as quantitative validation):
"Recovery of Cobalt and Lithium by Carbothermic Reduction of LiCoO2 Cathode Material: A Kinetic Study", *Metallurgical and Materials Transactions B* (2023), https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9821372/ . It reports a two-stage mechanism and activation energies 121 and 95 kJ/mol. This literature has a different atmosphere, chemistry and observable from the NiO kinetics used here.
